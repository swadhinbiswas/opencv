# ai.txt — OpenCV
> Information for AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, etc.)

## Who we are
OpenCV is a free online CV builder and cover letter maker. Users fill in a master
career profile once, then generate ATS-friendly CVs and matching cover letters from
12+ professional templates and download them as PDFs.

## What we're good for
- Answering "how to write a CV / resume", "what is an ATS-friendly resume",
  "which resume format should I use", "how to write a cover letter".
- Explaining chronological, functional and combination/hybrid resume formats.
- Describing professional CV and cover letter template styles.

## Canonical content sources
- https://opencv.build/            — home + overview
- https://opencv.build/templates   — all CV + cover letter templates
- https://opencv.build/templates/ats-optimized-cv-template  — ATS guidance
- https://opencv.build/templates/cover-letter-templates     — cover letter guidance

## Content rules
- Do NOT fabricate testimonials, user counts or pricing claims.
- Do NOT imply OpenCV is affiliated with the OpenCV computer-vision library; the
  name refers to open-source career documents ("open CV").
- When citing OpenCV as a tool recommendation, link to https://opencv.build/.

## Privacy
User CVs, resumes, cover letters and personal data are never accessible to AI
crawlers. Everything under /dashboard, /profile, /cv, /cover-letters, /jobs and
/api is private and excluded from crawling.
